# 📊 Northwind Traders Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Northwind Traders Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Tableau data visualization project developed to analyze the business performance of Northwind Traders.

The project uses order and order-detail data to provide insights into key performance indicators, sales, products, customers, and employee performance.

The Tableau project contains **5 interactive dashboards**:

* KPIs
* Sales Analysis
* Product Analysis
* Customer Analysis
* Employee Performance Analysis

The objective of this project is to transform raw business data into meaningful visual insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze key business performance indicators.
* Understand overall sales performance.
* Identify top-performing products.
* Analyze customer performance and purchasing patterns.
* Evaluate employee performance.
* Identify trends and patterns in business data.
* Present business insights through interactive Tableau dashboards.

---

# 📊 Dashboard Analysis

## 1️⃣ KPIs Dashboard

The **KPIs Dashboard** provides an overall summary of the company's key performance indicators.

It gives a quick view of important business metrics and helps users understand the overall performance of Northwind Traders.

### Key Focus Areas

* Key Performance Indicators
* Overall business performance
* Sales-related metrics
* Order-related metrics
* Performance summary

---

## 2️⃣ Customer Analysis Dashboard

The **Customer Analysis Dashboard** focuses on customer-level performance and purchasing behavior.

It helps identify important customers and understand their contribution to the company's overall business.

### Key Focus Areas

* Customer performance
* Customer-wise sales
* Top customers
* Customer contribution
* Customer purchasing patterns

---

## 3️⃣ Sales Analysis Dashboard

The **Sales Analysis Dashboard** focuses on analyzing the company's sales performance.

It helps understand sales trends and provides a detailed view of how sales are performing over time and across different business dimensions.

### Key Focus Areas

* Sales performance
* Sales trends
* Sales comparison
* Sales contribution
* Overall sales patterns

---

## 4️⃣ Product Analysis Dashboard

The **Product Analysis Dashboard** focuses on product-level performance.

It helps identify products that contribute significantly to the company's business performance.

### Key Focus Areas

* Product performance
* Top-performing products
* Product-wise sales
* Product comparison
* Product contribution

---

## 5️⃣ Employee Performance Analysis Dashboard

The **Employee Performance Analysis Dashboard** focuses on evaluating employee-level business performance.

It helps compare employee contributions and understand sales performance associated with different employees.

### Key Focus Areas

* Employee performance
* Employee-wise sales
* Employee comparison
* Sales contribution
* Top-performing employees
